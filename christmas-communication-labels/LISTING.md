## Full description
The holidays bring new words, new routines and a lot of noise, lights and people. These **Christmas Communication Labels** give your autistic learners and AAC users a clear, calm way to take part: they can request, comment, share feelings and ask for a break.

**What's included (23 pages):**
- **64 picture cards** in 5 color-coded groups:
  - Sentence starters: I want, I see, I like, I feel, I need
  - Christmas things: Christmas tree, Santa, present, stocking, star, ornament, candy cane, snowman, reindeer, cookie, gingerbread, lights, wreath, bell, elf, sleigh, hot cocoa, snow, mittens, milk
  - Christmas actions: open, wrap, decorate, bake, sing, give, eat, drink, look, play
  - Feelings & senses: happy, excited, calm, sad, mad, scared, surprised, tired, overwhelmed, too loud, too bright, cold
  - Social words & needs: yes, no, help, break please, all done, more, wait, my turn, headphones, quiet, hug, no hug please, high five, thank you, Merry Christmas, bathroom, go home
- **Large labels** (6 per page) for labeling the room, word walls and pocket charts
- **Small cards** (20 per page) for PECS-style exchange, binders and lanyards
- **Blank cards** for adding your own photos of family members, toys or routines
- **Christmas Talk Board**: a one-page core board for modeling
- **Feelings & Needs Board**: keep it handy at parties, concerts and family visits
- **Sentence strips**: I want / I see / I like / I feel / I need
- **First / Then board** and a **"When Christmas feels like too much, I can…" calm choices board**
- Teacher guide with prep steps, ideas for use and a color key

**Why teachers love it**
- Clean, uncluttered pictures on white backgrounds, with one idea per card
- Fitzgerald-Key-style color coding, so the cards fit right in with AAC systems
- Sensory and self-advocacy cards (too loud, too bright, headphones, break please)
- Consent-friendly greeting choices (hug, no hug please, high five)
- Clear, literacy-friendly font
- Low prep: print, laminate, cut and add hook-and-loop dots

**Perfect for:** special education classrooms, autism support classrooms, speech therapy (SLPs), early intervention, preschool, inclusion classrooms and families at home.
