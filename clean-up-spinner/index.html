    Put the spinner on your home screen

    1. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add. The spinner opens full screen from its new icon, even without Wi-Fi.

    Teacher setup

    Children in my class
    16

    Type your own area names, pick a picture, and set how many helpers each area needs. The wheel changes as you type. Bigger areas come up more often, so every spot gets filled.

    Add your students and the spinner calls each child up by name, then shows where everyone is helping. Leave the list empty to spin for spots only.

    Who spins next

    If a child is out today, tap Away today under the wheel. They'll be skipped until you start a new clean-up.
