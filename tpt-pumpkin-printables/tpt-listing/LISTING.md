## Pricing suggestion

I couldn't find reliable live TpT prices, so treat this as a starting point and check the "Best Selling" sort for "pumpkin craft bundle" before you list. On other stores, single fall crafts sell for around $3-$5, and TpT-style marketplace category bands commonly put seasonal bundles in the $10-$20+ range.

- **Bundle (all 50 + app):** list at **$18-$24**. TpT sales and promo days take a percentage off, so set the base price with that in mind.
- **Individual PDFs:** $3.00-$3.50 each (see `individual-listings.csv`). Listing the singles as well as the bundle gives you 50 more search entry points, and each single can link to the bundle.
- **Mini bundles (optional):** "Pumpkin Character Crafts" (#10-22), "Pumpkin Math" and "Pumpkin Literacy" at $8-$12 each.
- **Freebie (optional):** offer #8 Real Pumpkin Face Kit or #5 Pumpkin Feelings free, to bring in followers and reviews.

## Before you publish: checklist

- [ ] Set your store name: change `STORE_NAME` in `generator/layout.py`, then rebuild (see the README). Every footer, terms page and preview watermark updates automatically.
- [ ] Open a few PDFs and print one test page at "Actual size".
- [ ] Unzip the upload file and double-click `Classroom-App/index.html` to check that the app opens.
- [ ] Upload the 4 thumbnails in order (1-cover first, because it becomes the search result image).
- [ ] Upload `PREVIEW-watermarked.pdf` as the preview.
- [ ] Add a link to your store's other fall products in the description once they exist.
- [ ] Release it early in the season (late August to September). Fall resources sell best before October.
